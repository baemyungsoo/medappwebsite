Services

Built around how care actually happens

Four foundations, each designed with clinical care teams and everyday caregivers in mind.

Smart Session Scheduling

cura groups every medication into natural morning, afternoon, and evening sessions — one clear moment to act on, not a scattered list of individual pills.

Verified Drug Safety Checks

Every medication is checked against a verified pharmaceutical registry, so allergy conflicts and duplicate ingredients are flagged by active ingredient — not just by brand name.

Adherence Insights

Clear, visual adherence history shows what was taken, missed, or skipped over time — insight that's simple to review together at the next appointment.

Family Care Coordination

Manage schedules for a parent, a child, or anyone in your care from a single account, with each person's routine kept separate, private, and clear.
